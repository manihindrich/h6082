#__ About this board
## ☕ Demo use case: a café team's board
![Café U Mlýnku](/media/demo-cafe.svg)

**Café U Mlýnku** is run by Lucie with six people: three baristas, a baker and two part-timers. The board runs on a tablet behind the bar and everyone has it on their phone:

- **whoever opens** goes through *Opening* and ticks it off, **whoever closes** does *Closing*. The next day everyone can see what was done.
- **shifts** and notes for the next shift are edited right on the page with ✎ (`##*` blocks)
- **drink recipes** and procedures (dialling in, milk, filter, cold brew) are the same for everyone, so the taste doesn't depend on who is behind the bar
- **a new colleague** follows *Onboarding* step by step and finds documents and numbers in one place
- **colleagues from abroad** get the same board in English, the team switches the language. Czech: [Czech version](/demo-cafe?lang=cs)

> [!warning] Internal page
> Phone numbers and documents are for the team only: run the board behind a login. Alarm codes, the till PIN and passwords never belong here.

## ☕ Drinks
| drink | espresso | milk | cup |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| espresso | 18 g → 36 g, 27–30 s | — | 80 ml |
| doppio | 18 g → 36 g, full yield | — | 80 ml |
| cappuccino | 1 shot | 150 ml, 1 cm foam | 180 ml |
| flat white | ristretto 18 g → 28 g | 120 ml, thin foam | 160 ml |
| latte | 1 shot | 250 ml | glass 300 ml |
| cortado | 1 shot | 1 : 1 warm milk | glass 120 ml |
| gingerbread latte | 1 shot + 15 ml syrup | 250 ml | glass 300 ml |

??? tip "Dialling in espresso"
    **Runs fast (under 25 s), tastes sour** → grind finer.
    **Runs slow (over 32 s), tastes bitter** → grind coarser.
    Keep the 18 g dose, adjust only the grind. Two test shots after every change. Brew temperature 93 °C.

## 🥛 Milk
1. cold milk from the fridge, jug filled to a third
2. wand just under the surface, stretch until the jug feels lukewarm (about 37 °C)
3. sink the wand and spin the milk to 60–65 °C, the jug is hot to the touch
4. tap on the counter, swirl, glossy surface without bubbles
5. plant milk only to 55 °C or it splits

## 💧 V60 filter
1. 15 g of coffee, grind like coarse salt, water at 94 °C, 250 g in total
2. rinse the paper filter with hot water, discard the water
3. bloom: 40 g of water, wait 30 s
4. pour slowly in circles to 150 g, then to 250 g
5. done in 2:30–3:00. Faster = grind finer, slower = coarser.

## 🧊 Cold brew
100 g of coarse coffee per 1 l of cold water, 16–18 h in the fridge, strain through a filter.
Served diluted 1 : 1 with water or milk, over ice.
==Always label the container with the date, it keeps for 5 days.==
